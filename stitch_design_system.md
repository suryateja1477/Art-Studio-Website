# Design System: The Artisan Studio Atelier

## 1. Brand Identity & Creative North Star
"The Living Atelier" — an earthy, high-end fine art gallery experience that celebrates craftsmanship, tactile textures, and natural pigments. The aesthetic balances contemporary editorial restraint with classical warmth, moving away from sterile ecommerce grids into a curated exhibition.

## 2. Color Palette & Atmospheric Depth
- Canvas Background: Linen Cream (#FCFAF7)
- Card Surfaces: Soft Parchment (#F5EFE6) and Warm Alabaster (#FFFFFF)
- Primary Accent: Terracotta Red Ochre (#B85D38)
- Secondary Accent: Deep Forest Jade (#2D5A4C)
- Warm Accent: Raw Sienna (#D4A373)
- Text Primary: Charcoal Mineral (#1F1B18)
- Text Muted: Earth Umber (#6B635B)
- Status Available: Jade Evergreen (#2E7D32)
- Status Sold Out: Burnt Sienna (#C62828)

## 3. Typography: Editorial Sophistication
- Headlines & Display: 'Playfair Display', serif, font-weight 700 / 600, tight tracking (-0.02em)
- Body & Functional UI: 'Plus Jakarta Sans', sans-serif, font-weight 400 / 500 / 600, line-height 1.6
- Accents & Metadata: Uppercase letter-spaced badges (+0.08em), 12px, font-weight 700

## 4. Elevation, Radii & Tactile Borders
- Corner Radii: 16px (cards), 24px (featured panels), 9999px (pills and primary buttons)
- Subtle Layering: Soft diffused drop shadows (0 10px 30px rgba(74, 53, 38, 0.06))
- Borders: 1px subtle sandstone stroke (rgba(184, 93, 56, 0.12))
- Glassmorphism: Fixed header with 75% linen transparency and 20px blur

## 5. Core Application Views & Components
1. **Curated Art Gallery (Paintings):**
   - High-resolution artwork cards with floating availability badges, artist notes, medium/dimensions, price, and instant checkout.
2. **Wall Mural Commission Booking:**
   - Visual dimension calculator (10 to 500 sq.ft at ₹150/sq.ft), interactive date picker, execution calendar, and dynamic cost estimation.
3. **Custom Pottery Studio:**
   - Artisanal pottery vessel selector (Kulhad, Surahi, Vase, Bowl, Chai Cup), glaze sample selector (Kumkum Red, Indigo Blue, Mustard Ochre, Forest Green, Slate Ash), quantity selector, and lead-time indicators.
4. **Studio Dashboard & Order History:**
   - Status tracking badges (Requested, Confirmed, Declined, Cancelled) and seamless administration actions.
